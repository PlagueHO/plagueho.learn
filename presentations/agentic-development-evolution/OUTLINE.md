## Objectives

1. Recognize the shift from code completion to connected, delegated, and governed agentic work.
1. Identify the Frustration Barrier and the capabilities required to cross it.
1. Use the adoption journey to choose the next organizational capability rather than the next isolated feature.
1. Understand how Hypervelocity Engineering extends AI participation across the delivery system.

## Narrative

The deck follows one adoption curve:

**Complete → Delegate → Orchestrate → Standardize → Govern → Extend**

The central argument is that maturity is not measured by generated code. It is measured by faster, safer feedback loops across more roles.

## Adoption journey

1. Individual Developer Productivity
1. Frustration Barrier
1. Context engineering
1. Skill Development
1. Plugins & Marketplaces
1. Governed Team Scale
1. Business Barrier
1. Hypervelocity Engineering

Governed Team Scale is the bridge between shared assets and organization-wide flow. It requires paved workflows, evaluation and observability, security policy, ownership, and adoption measures.

The Business Barrier highlights the constraints that emerge next: product, operations, and security must keep pace with delivery; and greater output must still produce the right customer outcomes.
